Shorts Hub

Shorts Hub is a private tool used by its owner, Maksim Sidelskij, to publish short animated educational videos to his own YouTube channels: Deep Sea Files, Myst3ry Files and Money Facts.

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and publishing times on those channels. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
